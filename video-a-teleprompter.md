## About the date-stamp idea

Skip it on this one. That works for a YouTuber because episodic content gets watched within days of upload — the timestamp signals freshness. This video is a permanent asset on your site and in outbound. "Happy Monday, it's the 17th" reads as charming today and abandoned in six months, and it spends five seconds of the first ten, which is where you lose the most viewers.

**Where it genuinely works: personalized outbound.** If you record a 45-second version for a specific prospect, open with the date and their company name — *"Hey Sarah, it's Monday morning, I just looked at your careers page and you've got six roles open."* There the timestamp is the whole point: it proves the video was made for them, that day. Different asset, different rules. Worth doing once this one is shot.
