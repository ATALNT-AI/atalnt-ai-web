# FOR DANIEL, BEFORE HE RECORDS

**Say "we," never "I."** He's on the team, so "we found him" is accurate. He must not
imply he personally ran the search or set the fee — that was Nik's placement.

**Slow down on the numbers.** Every figure gets its own beat. Rushing $32,500 wastes
the only hard number in the video.

**Do not name the client, the state, the project, or the candidate.** "A commercial
construction firm in the Southeast" is as specific as it goes.

**The honest line is load-bearing.** "ATALNT wouldn't have kept him in that job" is
not a weakness — it's what makes the rest believable. Any prospect who has recruited
knows no system stops someone leaving for personal reasons. Deliver it straight, not
apologetically.

**Verify before publishing:** $130,000 × 25% = $32,500. Confirm against the actual
invoice, since the figure was recalled as "thirty, thirty-two thousand."
