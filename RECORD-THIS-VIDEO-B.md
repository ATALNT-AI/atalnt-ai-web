# VIDEO B — PRODUCT WALKTHROUGH

## Record in two passes

**Pass 1 — screen only, no talking.** Drive the product through the six steps below.
Go slow. Half your natural speed. Pause a beat on every screen before you click.

**Pass 2 — voice only.** Read the script over the footage.

Trying to click and narrate at once is why demos come out stiff. Two passes takes
ten minutes longer and sounds twice as good.

---

# PASS 1 · SHOT LIST

Read this before you record. Don't read it on camera.

**1 · ROLE** — Open a new role. Paste the Plant Manager job description in raw, messy.
Let it structure. Answer the clarifying questions. Land on the finished role brief.

**2 · CANDIDATES** — Open the ranked shortlist. Scroll slowly top to bottom. Stop.

**3 · INTELLIGENCE** — Click into Michael R. Show the match breakdown —
role fit, experience, skills, leadership. Let the scores sit on screen.

**4 · ENGAGEMENT** — Show the outreach view. The channels. Open one personalized message.
Show a real reply thread if you have one.

**5 · SCREENING** — Open the screening card. Score, skills assessment, experience,
culture, references.

**6 · INTERVIEW** — Show the scheduled interview. Date, time, with hiring manager.
End on that screen and hold it.

**Hold each screen ~4 seconds longer than feels natural.** You need room to talk over it.
