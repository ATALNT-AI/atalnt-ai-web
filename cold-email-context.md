# ATALNT AI Cold Email Context Brief

Single source of truth for any cold email agent writing ATALNT AI campaigns.
Compiled 2026-08-18 from the live site code, the sales playbook, and the
outreach strategy sessions. If an email contradicts this file, the email is wrong.

---

## 1. Voice rules (these outrank everything below)

- **Never use an em dash. Not once.** Use commas, periods, colons, or rewrite.
  This is the number one house rule and the most reliable AI tell.
- Kill on sight: "I hope this email finds you well", "just checking in",
  buzzwords, generic praise, anything over 150 words when shorter works.
- Research before writing. Every email opens with a specific, verifiable fact
  about the prospect: their posted recruiter req, its age, their open role count,
  recent news. If the merge fields cannot be filled, do not send.
- One clear, low friction ask per email. Either/or asks convert best.
- Subject lines: lowercase, specific, no brackets, no "quick question".
- Signature on every cold email:
  Nik Jain / Co-Founder, COO / ATALNT | Talent + Technology Solutions / (214) 842-1104
- Never invent a candidate, a client, a reference, or a shared background.
- Full playbook: sales-playbook.md in this repo.

## 2. What ATALNT AI is (current, AI-first framing)

A subscription recruiting service. **The AI works your searches around the
clock, and you get direct access to a dedicated recruiter who oversees every
one.** Sourced candidate list within a week of intake.

- The AI does the volume work: sourcing across seven channels (internal and
  external databases, LinkedIn, job postings, email, voice, and text campaigns),
  screening, ranking.
- The human is called a **dedicated recruiter** (retitled from "account
  manager", Aug 2026). They are oversight and a person to reach. Do NOT credit
  the recruiter with doing the sourcing/screening by hand; that undersells the
  engine. Do NOT list their tasks exhaustively or quote hours per week.
- Interview scheduling handled, offer delivery and negotiation included.
- Tagline: "AI recruiting with a human on your side."

  subscription ends, with no extra fee.
- Break-even honesty: under roughly 3 hires a year an agency is cheaper and we
  say so. This admission is load-bearing for credibility.

## 4. The target and the argument

**List trigger:** US companies, any industry, 100 to 2,000 employees, with an
open posting for an internal recruiter (Recruiter, Corporate Recruiter, TA
Specialist/Partner/Manager, Technical Recruiter, Sourcer, Head of TA).
Exclude staffing/recruiting/RPO/PEO firms, existing clients, and CRM contacts.

**Contact:** HR/People leaders, Director and above. Under ~200 employees with
no HR leader, go to COO/CFO/founder. Never open on an IC recruiter.

**Qualifier:** their TOTAL open req count, not the recruiter req.
5 roles = Core. 6 to 10 = Growth. 11+ = Custom (internal sizing only). Say "at least [N] roles
posted", never state their count as fact (postings undercount and include
evergreens).

**The enemy is the in-house build, not agency fees.** This list is hiring a
recruiter, so the comparison is:

Year one for a $150k recruiter: $150,000 comp + $37,500 burden (25%) +
$10,800 LinkedIn Recruiter + $14,000 sourcing data + $6,000 job boards +
$4,000 outreach tools = **$222,300 before a single candidate is sourced.**
At the US average recruiter salary the same stack totals $146,393.

**The strongest cost line (without naming our price): the tool stack alone
runs about $34,800 a year, and our subscription costs less than that
recruiter's tools, let alone the recruiter.**

Plus time: 60 to 90 days to fill the recruiter seat, ~30 more to ramp, while
every other req sits still. We deliver a sourced candidate list inside a week.

**Never position as "instead of the recruiter."** The buyer just won that
headcount fight. We compete with the ninety days before the hire starts and
the tooling budget, not with the hire. "Keep your search running. This just
means the reqs do not wait for it."

## 5. Segments and openers

- **Aged req (45+ days posted):** best segment. Lead with the day count.
  5-touch sequence.
- **Fresh req (under 21 days):** never imply their search will drag. Lead with
  "while you are hiring for [title]". 3 touches.
- **First recruiter ever:** no TA function exists, no job-threat sensitivity.
  Lead with the $222k build cost.
- **Pay-transparency states (CA, CO, NY, WA, IL...):** the salary range is in
  their own posting. Use THEIR number in the math email. Highest converting.
- Verify the req is still live before sending. Stop the sequence on any reply.

## 6. Numbers safe to use

- "18 to 25 percent of first-year salary" for agency fees ("20 percent" is
  what we quote on calls)
- "$10,000 to $40,000 per hire" for agency fees in dollars
- "Sourced candidate list within a week of intake"
- "Less than half the cost of a recruiter, fully loaded"
- The recruiter-stack table in section 4 (sourced: Salary.com, Indeed,
  Glassdoor, published list pricing)

## 7. Never say (each of these has burned someone)

- Any dollar figure for our plans. Pricing is custom and quoted per account.
- Any claim about where the team is located (not US-based, not in-house,
  not onshore). Approved wording: "a dedicated recruiter".
- "Cancel anytime" or "month to month" (90-day minimum now applies).
- "SOC 2 certified/compliant". Only: "SOC 2 Type II audit in progress."
- "2.4M profiles indexed" (unsubstantiated).
- "30+ job boards" (count unverified).
- Any guarantee a role gets filled or an offer accepted.
- "Replace your recruiting team", "recruiting on autopilot".
- Named clients or testimonials (attribution unconfirmed).
- Any days-to-fill or time-to-hire statistic.
- Contingent/placement-fee terms (25%, replacement guarantee, exclusivity
  discounts). Those belong to ATALNT LLC's staffing business, not this product.

## 8. Compliance floor

- CAN-SPAM: accurate From/Reply-To, truthful subject, physical postal address
  in footer, working opt-out honored within 10 business days.
- Suppress on request permanently, account-level, shared list.
- Verified addresses only. Stop on reply. No lookalike domains, no fake
  "re:" threads.
- No cold texting, no ringless voicemail. Text only after a reply or booking.
