# Ad audio

Drop two files here and the ad at `/ad` picks them up automatically:

- `vo.mp3` — the voiceover, recorded against the 58-second script in `promo-ad-script.md`
- `music.mp3` — the music bed

Neither is required. Missing files are reported in the control bar and the ad
plays silent.

Both tracks are locked to the page's master clock, so scrubbing the timeline
seeks the audio too.

**The music bed is faded in at 0:24, on the turn, and sits at 26% under the
voice.** It is silent for the whole problem half. That is deliberate — the
contrast between the near-silent first 24 seconds and the music entering on
"That's not a hiring problem" is doing more work than any single line of copy.
If you replace the track, keep that entry point.

Until a real track exists, tick **Scratch pad** in the control bar for a
synthesised placeholder bed so you can hear the timing. It is a scratch
reference, not a score — license a real track before this ships.
